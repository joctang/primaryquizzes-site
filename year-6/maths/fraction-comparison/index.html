    For parents: how this set helps

    This set practises Comparing and ordering fractions for Year 6. Our Year 6 sets target order of operations, fractions and decimals, measurement, statistics, plus advanced comprehension, spelling and grammar.

    Every practice set is written against the Australian Curriculum v9.0 content descriptions for its year level, so what your child practises here matches what they are taught in class. Year 6 is the last primary year and the launchpad for Year 7. There is no primary NAPLAN this year, so it is the best window for stretching practice at each child's own pace.

    Sit nearby for the first round if you like, then let your child work alone – the explanation after each answer does the teaching. About five minutes per round, and no account needed.
