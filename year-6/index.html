Year 6 practice by subject and skill

Explore Year 6 practice by subject and skill, with short quizzes and answer explanations for the topics currently available.

Current Year 6 sets include fractions, decimals, percentages, measurement, space, data, chance, spelling, grammar, vocabulary and reading. Each set is a small practice sample, not a full assessment of Year 6 learning.

Some sets include Australian Curriculum v9 codes to show their intended curriculum links. Australian states and territories plan their own teaching sequences, and a short quiz cannot represent a whole year of learning. These sample sets are still awaiting the site's planned cross-AI content review.
