    About this Year 6 vocabulary practice set

    This set focuses on Vivid, emotive and figurative vocabulary. Use the answer explanations to review each question; a short quiz is a practice sample, not a full test or assessment.

    Curriculum codes describe intended links and do not mean this set covers a full year level or every state's teaching sequence. This sample is awaiting the site's planned cross-AI content review.
