Roots that give clues

A familiar root can hint at meaning, but it does not explain every part of a word’s spelling or pronunciation.

  • tele (far): telephone, telescope, television
  • photo (light): photograph, photosynthesis
  • bio (life): biology, biography, biodiversity
  • graph (write or draw): graphic, paragraph, autograph
  • port (carry): transport, portable, import

A simple way to practise

How to practise Year 6 spelling

The list focuses on longer and less familiar words. Breaking a word into meaningful parts, noticing repeated letter patterns and checking it in a sentence can make its spelling easier to recall.

  • Say the word, then look for smaller familiar parts without assuming every part is pronounced as it is written.
  • Write a tricky word from memory, compare it with the correct spelling, and practise the part you missed.
  • Use the word in a sentence to connect its spelling with its meaning.
