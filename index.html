Get in touch
Is Primary Quizzes free?

Yes. Every practice set is free, with no sign-up, no account and no app to install. Open a page, answer 10 questions, and read the explanation for each answer.

Can this help with NAPLAN?

NAPLAN is taken in Years 3, 5, 7 and 9. Our Year 3 and Year 5 quizzes offer short practice in selected skills, but they are not official NAPLAN materials or full practice tests, and we cannot predict results.

How does this relate to the Australian Curriculum?

Some sets show Australian Curriculum v9 codes as intended links. States and territories organise teaching differently, and the codes do not mean a set covers the full curriculum. These sample sets are awaiting the site's planned cross-AI review.

How long does one practice round take?

Each round has 10 questions with an explanation after every answer. The time depends on the learner and the questions.

Does my child need an account? Do you collect data?

No account is needed. Quiz progress stays on the device. See the Privacy page for details about information handled when someone contacts us or chooses to send feedback.

Can teachers use these in class?

Teachers and families can use a set to practise one listed skill. The short quizzes are supplementary practice, not a complete curriculum resource or assessment.

Short practice, together or solo

Short rounds can fit into a family routine: after school, on the weekend or on a tablet at the kitchen table. Children can work on their own – every question explains its answer – or you can sit beside them and talk it through. Nothing to install, nothing to pay, nothing to sign.
