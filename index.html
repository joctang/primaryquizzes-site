Free practice for Australian primary school, aligned to the curriculum

Every practice set is written against the Australian Curriculum v9.0 content descriptions for its year level, so what your child practises here matches what they are taught in class.

NAPLAN sits in Years 3, 5, 7 and 9. Our Year 3 and Year 5 sets build exactly the number, reading and language skills those tests measure – in a friendly, no-timer format that keeps practice something children choose to do, not something they dread.

What your child practises, year by year

Year 2 · Early Stage 1 into Stage 1

In Year 2, children move from counting to working confidently with two- and three-digit numbers, telling time, and reading short texts on their own. It is also the year when good habits – reading the question carefully, checking an answer – start to stick.

Our Year 2 sets focus on place value, addition and subtraction strategies, fractions of shapes, telling the time, and early comprehension and spelling patterns.

The first NAPLAN sits in Year 3, so Year 2 is the ideal time to build the number fluency and reading comprehension that test measures – without any test pressure at all.

See Year 2 practice sets

Year 3 · Stage 1, moving into Stage 2

Year 3 is a jump in independence: multiplication tables, money and measurement in maths, and longer texts with inference and vocabulary work in English. Children who practise little and often tend to handle the step up easily.

Our Year 3 sets cover the times tables, addition and subtraction beyond 100, angles as turns, money in dollars and cents, plus spelling, grammar and reading comprehension.

NAPLAN happens in Year 3 – most children meet it as their first formal test. Short, friendly practice rounds like these make the format familiar long before the week arrives.

See Year 3 practice sets

Year 4 · Stage 2

In Year 4, the focus shifts to applying skills: multi-step word problems, equivalent fractions, and reading for meaning rather than decoding. It is a consolidating year between the first NAPLAN in Year 3 and the next one in Year 5.

Our Year 4 sets target multi-digit multiplication and division, fractions, area and perimeter, data and graphs, along with spelling patterns and grammar.

There is no NAPLAN in Year 4, which makes it the calmest year to close any gaps – especially times tables and reading comprehension – before Year 5.

See Year 4 practice sets

Year 5 · Stage 2, moving into Stage 3

Year 5 brings bigger numbers, decimals and percentage ideas in maths, and in English, comparing texts, inferring and editing writing. Work becomes more abstract, and practice matters more than ever.

Our Year 5 sets cover fractions, decimals, measurement conversions, angles and data, together with comprehension, vocabulary in context, spelling and punctuation.

NAPLAN returns in Year 5, now with questions drawn from across three years of learning. Regular short rounds build both the skills and the test stamina.

See Year 5 practice sets

Year 6 · Stage 3

Year 6 is the bridge to secondary school: order of operations, integers and rates in maths, and analysing texts and structuring writing in English. Confidence here carries directly into Year 7.

Our Year 6 sets target order of operations, fractions and decimals, measurement, statistics, plus advanced comprehension, spelling and grammar.

Year 6 is the last primary year and the launchpad for Year 7. There is no primary NAPLAN this year, so it is the best window for stretching practice at each child's own pace.

See Year 6 practice sets

Questions parents ask

Is Primary Quizzes free?

Yes. Every practice set is free, with no sign-up, no account and no app to install. Open a page, answer 10 questions, and read the explanation for each answer.

Does this help with NAPLAN?

NAPLAN tests Years 3, 5, 7 and 9. Our Year 3 and Year 5 sets build the same number, reading and language skills in a low-pressure format, so the real test week feels familiar rather than scary.

Is the practice aligned to the Australian Curriculum?

Yes. Each set is written against the Australian Curriculum v9.0 content descriptions for its year level, from Year 2 through Year 6.

How long does one practice round take?

About five minutes. Ten questions with instant explanations – short enough for after school, a car trip or the weekend, and frequent enough to build real fluency.

Does my child need an account? Do you collect data?

No. There are no accounts and nothing to fill in. Progress stays on your device and none of the questions ask for personal information.

Can teachers use these in class?

Yes. Sets work well as warm-ups, revision or homework links. Every question comes with an explanation you can talk through together.

Five minutes of practice, together or solo

Short rounds fit real family life: after school, on the weekend, on a tablet at the kitchen table. Children can work on their own – every question explains its answer – or you can sit beside them and talk it through. Nothing to install, nothing to pay, nothing to sign.
