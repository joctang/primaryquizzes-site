Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Primary Quizzes is made for children, so we collect as little as we can. This page explains in plain language what happens to information when you use the site.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no sign-up, and we never ask for a child's name, age or school.
  • There is currently no advertising or analytics. The site does not use behavioural advertising or tracking tools. Our hosting provider receives technical information needed to deliver and protect the site.
  • The site doesn't use cookies.
  • Best scores stay on the device. Quiz feedback is currently switched off; answers and scores are not sent to us.
  • If an adult emails us, we use the message only to reply and to fix problems.

Who we are

Primary Quizzes (primaryquizzes.com) is run by Joctan Gonzalez Herrera, based in Spain. You can contact us at joctan.publi@gmail.com. The operator's postal address and tax identification number are listed in our Terms of Use.

What stays on your device

To show your best score, the site saves a small note in your own browser (using "local storage"). It contains only the name of the practice set, your best and latest score, how many rounds you have done, and the date of the latest round. This browser record stays on the device and is not linked to your name.

You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data. If you use a private window, nothing is kept. The practice works the same either way.

Cookies

We don't use cookies, so there is no cookie banner to accept or decline.

Technical information our web host receives

The site is hosted by Hostinger. When a page is requested, the hosting service may process technical details such as the IP address, requested page, date and time, and browser information for delivery, security and troubleshooting. We have not yet confirmed the exact log fields, storage location, retention period or relevant hosting subprocessors, so this site is not ready for general launch.

The hosting company keeps these logs for a limited period that it sets, and we use them only for security and to keep the site working. The server and backup locations must be confirmed with the hosting provider. We will update this notice with the verified details before general launch.

Children

Primary Quizzes is for children in primary school. We do not ask for a child's name, school or contact details. We suggest that an adult is nearby while children practise. The contact page is for parents, carers and teachers, so please don't ask a child to write to us.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to read and reply to you and to fix mistakes on the site. We don't share them. Keeping: up to 2 years after the last contact, then we delete them.

Sharing your information

We don't sell or share personal information. The only outside company that handles technical information is our web host, as described above. We may have to disclose information if the law requires it.

If this changes

If we ever add anything that affects privacy, such as analytics or advertising, we will update this page first and explain what changes before it is switched on. The date at the top shows when this page was last updated.

Your rights and questions

You can ask us what information we hold about you (for example, your emails), ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Please email joctan.publi@gmail.com.

If you are not happy with how we handled your information, please contact us first. In Australia you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au). Because we operate from Spain, European data protection law may also apply to us, and you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority (aepd.es).
