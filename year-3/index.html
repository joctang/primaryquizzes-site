Year 3 practice by subject and skill

Year 3 practice can include multiplication, money, measurement, reading and spelling. This page shows the topics covered by the sets currently available.

Current Year 3 sets include times tables, number, fractions, time, money, maps, data, chance, spelling, vocabulary and reading comprehension. Coverage varies by skill and is not a complete assessment.

NAPLAN: Year 3 is a NAPLAN year. These short, independent quizzes practise selected skills only; they are not official NAPLAN materials, a full practice test or a prediction of a result.

Some sets include Australian Curriculum v9 codes to show their intended curriculum links. Australian states and territories plan their own teaching sequences, and a short quiz cannot represent a whole year of learning. These sample sets are still awaiting the site's planned cross-AI content review.
