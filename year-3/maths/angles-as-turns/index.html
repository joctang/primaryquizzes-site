    For parents: how this set helps

    This set practises Recognising angles as measures of turn and comparing them with right angles for Year 3. Our Year 3 sets cover the times tables, addition and subtraction beyond 100, angles as turns, money in dollars and cents, plus spelling, grammar and reading comprehension.

    Every practice set is written against the Australian Curriculum v9.0 content descriptions for its year level, so what your child practises here matches what they are taught in class. NAPLAN happens in Year 3 – most children meet it as their first formal test. Short, friendly practice rounds like these make the format familiar long before the week arrives.

    Sit nearby for the first round if you like, then let your child work alone – the explanation after each answer does the teaching. About five minutes per round, and no account needed.
