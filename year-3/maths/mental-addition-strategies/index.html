    This set practises Using known addition and subtraction facts to calculate mentally with larger numbers for Year 3. Our Year 3 sets cover the times tables, addition and subtraction beyond 100, angles as turns, money in dollars and cents, plus spelling, grammar and reading comprehension.

    Every practice set is written against the Australian Curriculum v9.0 content descriptions for its year level, so what your child practises here matches what they are taught in class.
