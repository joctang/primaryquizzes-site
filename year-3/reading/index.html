A simple way to practise

How to practise Year 3 reading comprehension

Short passages help readers practise finding stated details, using clues and working out word meanings.

  • Read the question, then look back for the sentence that gives the answer.
  • For an inference, join a clue from the passage with what you already know.
  • Use nearby words and actions to work out an unfamiliar word.
