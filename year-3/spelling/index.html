How to practise Year 3 spelling

Year 3 practice covers longer words, less common letter patterns, word families and common words that can be tricky to remember. Work on one pattern at a time, then use the word in a sentence.

  • Break a longer word into syllables and listen for each sound, then check which letters represent it.
  • When adding an ending, find the base word first and notice whether its spelling changes.
  • For homophones, read the whole sentence and choose the spelling that matches the meaning.
