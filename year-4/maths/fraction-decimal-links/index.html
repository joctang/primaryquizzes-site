    For parents: how this set helps

    This set practises Equivalent fractions and decimal notation for Year 4. Our Year 4 sets target multi-digit multiplication and division, fractions, area and perimeter, data and graphs, along with spelling patterns and grammar.

    Every practice set is written against the Australian Curriculum v9.0 content descriptions for its year level, so what your child practises here matches what they are taught in class. There is no NAPLAN in Year 4, which makes it the calmest year to close any gaps – especially times tables and reading comprehension – before Year 5.

    Sit nearby for the first round if you like, then let your child work alone – the explanation after each answer does the teaching. About five minutes per round, and no account needed.
