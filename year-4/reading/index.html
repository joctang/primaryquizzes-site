How to practise Year 4 reading comprehension

These questions practise retrieving information, drawing conclusions from clues and identifying the main idea of a passage.

  • For a detail question, check the passage instead of relying on memory.
  • For an inference, find the clues that support your answer; it should fit more than one detail when possible.
  • The main idea covers most of the passage, while a small fact supports it.
