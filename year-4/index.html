Year 4 practice by subject and skill

Explore Year 4 practice by subject and skill, with short quizzes and explanations for the topics currently available on this site.

Current Year 4 sets include number, fractions, decimals, measurement, space, data, spelling, grammar, vocabulary and reading. Each set covers selected questions rather than a full year-level programme.

Some sets include Australian Curriculum v9 codes to show their intended curriculum links. Australian states and territories plan their own teaching sequences, and a short quiz cannot represent a whole year of learning. These sample sets are still awaiting the site's planned cross-AI content review.
