Contact

This page is for parents, carers and teachers. Please don't ask a child to write to us, and please don't include a child's name, school or other personal details in your message.

Email us

joctan.publi@gmail.com

There is no message form on this site. If feedback is enabled, you can also choose a quick feedback reason at the end of a practice set. We use your email address and message only to reply to you (see the Privacy Policy).

Found a mistake in a question?

Thank you, this helps a lot. To help us find it quickly, please tell us:

  • the name of the practice set (for example "Year 3 Spelling – Homophones") or the page address,
  • the question, copied as it appears,
  • what you think is wrong and, if you can, what it should say.

We check each report, correct the question if needed, and check it again.

Other questions

You can also write to us about privacy, to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted, or about anything else on the site. We read the messages we receive, but we can't promise a reply time.
