More Year 2 spelling words to practise

These extra examples give Year 2 learners more ways to practise. They are study prompts, not quiz questions or an official ACARA word list.

Common sounds

Say each word, listen for the sound, then look at the letters in brackets that represent it.

  • play, say, day, away (ay)
  • see, keep, three, sleep (ee)
  • high, light, might, right (igh)
  • road, coach, toast, soap (oa)
  • tail, main, paint, wait (ai)

Adding s and es

Listen to the ending of the word. Most words just add s; words that buzz or hiss add es.

  • cat → cats
  • dog → dogs
  • book → books
  • hand → hands
  • lunch → lunches
  • church → churches
  • wish → wishes
  • buzz → buzzes

How to practise Year 2 spelling

Year 2 practice covers common sounds, tricky words that are learned by looking, and simple word endings. Short, regular practice works best: say the word, write it, then check it.

  • Say the word slowly and listen for its sounds, then choose the letters that match them.
  • Some words, such as said and was, are not spelt the way they sound. Try look, cover, write, check.
  • When a word names more than one thing, listen for whether you add just s or the es sound.
