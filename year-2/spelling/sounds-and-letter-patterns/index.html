    About this Year 2 spelling practice set

    This set focuses on Letter patterns: digraphs, long vowels, silent letters. Use the answer explanations to review each question; a short quiz is a practice sample, not a full test or assessment.

    Intended curriculum links: AC9E2LY10.

    Curriculum codes describe intended links and do not mean this set covers a full year level or every state's teaching sequence. This sample is awaiting the site's planned cross-AI content review.

    Families and teachers can work through the questions together, or let the learner try a round independently and discuss the explanations afterwards. No account is needed.

    Free, independent practice for Australian primary school learners. Not affiliated with or endorsed by ACARA or NAPLAN.
