    For parents: how this set helps

    This set practises Letter patterns: digraphs, long vowels, silent letters for Year 2. Our Year 2 sets focus on place value, addition and subtraction strategies, fractions of shapes, telling the time, and early comprehension and spelling patterns.

    Every practice set is written against the Australian Curriculum v9.0 content descriptions for its year level, so what your child practises here matches what they are taught in class. The first NAPLAN sits in Year 3, so Year 2 is the ideal time to build the number fluency and reading comprehension that test measures – without any test pressure at all.

    Sit nearby for the first round if you like, then let your child work alone – the explanation after each answer does the teaching. About five minutes per round, and no account needed.
