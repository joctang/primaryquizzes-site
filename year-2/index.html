Year 2 practice by subject and skill

Year 2 practice can revisit number, measurement, time and early English skills. The topics available here depend on the practice sets listed for this year.

Current Year 2 sets include place value, addition and subtraction, fractions, time, spelling and early reading skills. Each set focuses on a small sample of questions, not the whole year-level curriculum.

Some sets include Australian Curriculum v9 codes to show their intended curriculum links. Australian states and territories plan their own teaching sequences, and a short quiz cannot represent a whole year of learning. These sample sets are still awaiting the site's planned cross-AI content review.

Primary Quizzes is free, independent practice for Australian primary school students. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, ACARA or NAPLAN.
