A clear guide for families

What do children learn in Year 2 in Australia?

Curriculum, in plain English

The Australian Curriculum v9 Year 2 Maths achievement standard includes numbers to at least 1,000, place value, practical addition and multiplication problems, money, fractions, patterns, measurement, time, space and data. English also includes speaking, listening, reading and viewing, writing and spelling. The sets below sample some of these areas; for example, our place-value quiz focuses on numbers to 100 and our Reading area currently has one words-and-images activity.

Australian Curriculum content descriptions are a national reference, while states, territories and schools organise their teaching and assessment. A curriculum code beside a set indicates an intended link; it does not mean that the set covers the whole code or the Year 2 program. A question many parents ask

Does Year 2 have NAPLAN?

Year 2 is not a NAPLAN test year. NAPLAN is held in Years 3, 5, 7 and 9. Schools also use ongoing teacher assessment and their own reporting to follow a child's progress, so ask your child's teacher about the checks used at your school. Primary Quizzes is independent practice, not an official test or a prediction of school results.

For parents & carers

A few helpful answers

Understand what this practice is for, how a round works and where your child’s progress is stored.

What can my Year 2 child practise here?+

There are current Maths, Spelling, Reading and Vocabulary activities. The lists above show the specific skills available now; the sets sample skills rather than cover the entire Year 2 curriculum.

Does Year 2 have NAPLAN?+

No. NAPLAN is the national literacy and numeracy assessment for Years 3, 5, 7 and 9. Year 2 progress is also followed through ongoing school and teacher assessment; contact the school for its assessment and reporting approach.

Are these official tests or a full curriculum assessment?+

No. These are independent practice activities for selected skills. Some sets show intended Australian Curriculum v9 links, but a short quiz cannot assess a whole year level. The pilot questions are still awaiting the site's planned cross-AI content review.

How does a practice round work?+

A round contains 10 questions drawn from the selected set. After each answer, the activity shows an explanation. Children can practise at their own pace; there is no fixed time limit.

Is it free, and does my child need an account?+

The practice is free and does not require sign-up or an account. Quiz progress is stored on the device in that browser. See the Privacy page for details.
