About Primary Quizzes

What this site is

Primary Quizzes offers short, free practice quizzes in Maths and English for Australian primary school students. You choose a year and a skill, answer 10 questions, and see a clear explanation for each answer. There are no accounts and nothing to sign up for.

Who runs it

Primary Quizzes is an independent project run by Joctan Gonzalez Herrera, based in Spain. It is made for children and for the adults who help them learn.

You can read how we handle information in our Privacy Policy, and you can reach us on the Contact page.

How the questions are prepared

  1. 1Curriculum links are intended references. Some sets display Australian Curriculum v9 content-description codes to show the intended skill link. The codes do not mean that a set covers a full year level or matches every state's teaching sequence.
  2. 2Written with AI assistance. The questions and the explanations are written with the help of AI tools. We adapt and write practice questions ourselves; we don't copy official tests or published worksheets.
  3. 3Checked with automated tools. Automated checks look for broken questions, missing answers and repeated options, and for arithmetic slips in the maths explanations. They help, but they do not prove that every answer is right.
  4. 4Current review status. The planned cross-AI review has not been completed for the current sample sets. They may contain errors and are not approved as a complete curriculum resource. No teacher has reviewed them.

AI systems can make mistakes or share the same blind spots. Please use your own judgement and tell us if something looks wrong.

Free, with no ads

Primary Quizzes is free to use and currently has no advertising. If that ever changes, we will say so on this page and in the Privacy Policy before it happens.

Independent, and not a test

Primary Quizzes is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, ACARA, NAPLAN, or any school, education department or education publisher. It is practice, not an official test, and it can't predict or guarantee results in any test. This site is not connected with any book series or publication called "Primary Quizzes".
