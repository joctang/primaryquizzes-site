About Primary Quizzes

What this site is

Primary Quizzes offers short, free practice quizzes in Maths and English for Australian primary school students. You choose a year and a skill, answer 10 questions, and see a clear explanation for each answer. There are no accounts and nothing to sign up for.

Who runs it

Primary Quizzes is an independent project run by Joctan Gonzalez Herrera, based in Spain. It is made for children and for the adults who help them learn.

You can read how we handle information in our Privacy Policy, and you can reach us on the Contact page.

How the questions are made and checked

  1. 1Planned from the curriculum. Each practice set is planned with reference to specific content descriptions in the Australian Curriculum (Version 9), so it targets a real skill for that year level.
  2. 2Written with the help of AI. The questions and the explanations are written with the help of AI tools. We adapt and write practice questions ourselves; we don't copy official tests or published worksheets.
  3. 3Checked by computer. Automated checks look for broken questions, missing answers and repeated options, and for arithmetic slips in the maths explanations. They help, but they do not prove that every answer is right.
  4. 4Checked by several independent AI systems. Each practice set is then checked by several different AI systems. First, each one solves every question without seeing the answer. Then each one checks the answer and the explanation. Reviewers who disagree with the answer, or find the question confusing, send it back to be fixed and checked again. These checks were finished in October 2026 and cover the text of each question; pictures were checked through their written descriptions.
  5. 5Fixed when you tell us. If you find a mistake, please tell us. We check it, correct it, and check it again.

To be clear, these questions have not been reviewed by a teacher. AI systems can make mistakes or share the same blind spots, so please use your own judgement and tell us if something looks wrong.

Free, with no ads

Primary Quizzes is free to use and currently has no advertising. If that ever changes, we will say so on this page and in the Privacy Policy before it happens.
