    Helpful patterns

    • 2 times table: it's just doubling. 2 × 6 is double 6, which is 12.
    • 10 times table: every answer ends in 0. Put a 0 after the number you are multiplying.
    • 5 times table: every answer ends in 0 or 5. Count in fives like the minutes on a clock.
    • 4 times table: double the number, then double it again. 4 × 6: double 6 is 12, double 12 is 24.
    • 3 times table: double the number, then add one more of the number. 3 × 6: double 6 is 12, plus 6 is 18.
    • 9 times table: the digits of every answer add up to 9 (18 → 1 + 8, 27 → 2 + 7, 36 → 3 + 6). For 9 × 6, use 10 × 6 = 60, then take away 6 to get 54.
    • 6 times table: start with the 5 times table and add one more of the number. 6 × 7 is 5 × 7 = 35, plus 7, which is 42.
    • 8 times table: double, double and double again. 8 × 6: double 6 is 12, double is 24, double is 48.
    • 7 times table: use 5 × and 2 × facts together. 7 × 8 is 5 × 8 = 40, plus 2 × 8 = 16, which is 56.
    • Division is multiplication backwards: if 4 × 6 = 24, then 24 ÷ 4 = 6.
