    For parents: how this set helps

    This set practises Interpreting passages, vocabulary, text structure and purpose for Year 5. Our Year 5 sets cover fractions, decimals, measurement conversions, angles and data, together with comprehension, vocabulary in context, spelling and punctuation.

    Every practice set is written against the Australian Curriculum v9.0 content descriptions for its year level, so what your child practises here matches what they are taught in class. NAPLAN returns in Year 5, now with questions drawn from across three years of learning. Regular short rounds build both the skills and the test stamina.

    Sit nearby for the first round if you like, then let your child work alone – the explanation after each answer does the teaching. About five minutes per round, and no account needed.
