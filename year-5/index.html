Year 5 practice for parents: what to expect

Where Year 5 sits: Stage 2, moving into Stage 3. Year 5 brings bigger numbers, decimals and percentage ideas in maths, and in English, comparing texts, inferring and editing writing. Work becomes more abstract, and practice matters more than ever.

Our Year 5 sets cover fractions, decimals, measurement conversions, angles and data, together with comprehension, vocabulary in context, spelling and punctuation.

NAPLAN: NAPLAN returns in Year 5, now with questions drawn from across three years of learning. Regular short rounds build both the skills and the test stamina.

Every practice set is written against the Australian Curriculum v9.0 content descriptions for its year level, so what your child practises here matches what they are taught in class.
