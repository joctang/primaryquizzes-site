    For parents: how this set helps

    This set practises Connecting familiar percentages with fractions and decimals for Year 5. Our Year 5 sets cover fractions, decimals, measurement conversions, angles and data, together with comprehension, vocabulary in context, spelling and punctuation.

    Every practice set is written against the Australian Curriculum v9.0 content descriptions for its year level, so what your child practises here matches what they are taught in class.
